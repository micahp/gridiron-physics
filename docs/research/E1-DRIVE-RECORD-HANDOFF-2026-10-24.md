## E1 payload is NOT the E0a single-play envelope

- E1 `schema_version=1.0`, `mode=drive_statistical_demo`, `valid_full_game=false`, `simulation_id`, `seed`, `model_version`, `rules_version`, `start_state`, `plays[]`, `drive` summary, `provenance`, `outcome_pool`, `gaps`.
- The E0a payload has `mode=single_play_statistical_demo`, one play, and required `game_id`/`teams` and per-play `field.assignment_version=e0-field-end-v1` with source `authored_demo_convention`.
- E1 **does not supply all the same top-level fields** and instead has **multiple plays**, a **`drive` summary**, per-play **`sampling`**, expanded `outcome` flags and `field.assignment_version=e1-field-end-v2` / `source=authored_demo_orientation`. Do not coerce E1 into E0a or loosen E0a validation silently. Add a separate discriminator and explicit **research drive** validator/viewer profile; it is not shared v1 `pregame_full_game` conformance.
- E1 `event_type` vocabulary as implemented in generator: `run`, `pass_complete`, `pass_incomplete`, `sack`, `interception`, `fumble_lost`, `punt`, `field_goal_attempt`. **E0a has different names for several events** (e.g. `field_goal` versus E1 `field_goal_attempt`; `fumble` versus `fumble_lost`). Reject unknown kinds and agree an explicit versioned mapping, not a fallback to `run`.
- E1 **terminal vocabulary:** `touchdown`, `turnover`, `turnover_on_downs`, `punt`, `field_goal_attempt`, `safety`, `halftime`, `end_of_regulation`. In particular **`end_of_period` is superseded and must not appear in new E1 fixtures**. A Q1→Q2 or Q3→Q4 **`outcome.quarter_change=true` is NOT a terminal**; the next play remains on the same drive, no kickoff, offense retained, period clock resets and field orientation changes. A Q2 `halftime` is terminal for that half's drive, but **no second-half kickoff is yet generated**. A Q4 `end_of_regulation` is terminal for this research drive, but overtime/game outcome is unmodeled.
- Field: E1 has per-play `field.before` and `field.after` (`dir`, `los`); initial direction is an authored start convention, not a historically observed toss/end assignment. When possession changes, `dir` changes for the new possessing team. When Q1/Q3 ends, field direction changes **without changing possession**. Verify both LOS conversion formulas against the supplied state; require honest source labeling. At terminal events, `field.after.dir/los` may be null. Never fill unknown field positions or player identities.
- Summary: `drive` recomputed from plays (plays, yards, first downs, clock runoff, negative plays, turnover/punt/FG/quarter-change counts). **No team score, player stats, lineup, timeouts, penalties, complete kickoffs or verified as-of forecast**. Keep `outcome_pool.temporal_eligibility.status=retrospective` visible.
