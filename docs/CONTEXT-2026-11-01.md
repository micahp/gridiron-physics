# Context summary, 2026-11-01

Scope: the physics authority path in `engine/play.js`, run against BDB 2022 tracking for weeks 1 to 5, plus the NFL calibration harness. Weeks 6 to 9 stay reserved as the holdout and were not used. Nothing is committed or pushed.

## Correction to the earlier numbers today

Both harnesses measured run gain from the carrier's own start position, not from the line of scrimmage. On a handoff the back starts 4 to 7 yd behind the line, so every gain was inflated by that depth. The "physics runs are too long" finding was partly this artifact. Both harnesses now measure from the line, the origin NFL run yards use, and the numbers below replace the earlier ones.

`scripts/capture-movement.mjs` has the same origin problem in its absolute run yardage (`run_recorded_gain_yd_mean`, `runs_20plus_yd_*`). Its gain error is unaffected, because both sides share the origin. It is not fixed yet.

## What changed today

### Defense: assignments instead of a beeline at the ball

Before: when the handoff set the carrier, the run fits (`runfit`, `read`) switched to `pursue`, which aims at the carrier from wherever they stand. `contain` aimed at the carrier from its reaction time. Zone safeties ran to the catch point from anywhere on the field after every throw.

Now (`engine/play.js`):

- **Run fit** (`fit` brain, `fitLandmark`). Every run defender keys the back after his reaction delay (AWR), goes to his landmark, and takes an angle on the carrier only when the carrier is within 7 yd of him or past the line of scrimmage.
  - Linemen hold their own gap 1.2 yd into the backfield.
  - Middle and weak linebackers flow to the hole, 3.5 yd downfield.
  - The run-side corner works to the edge, 5 yd down and 16 yd wide. The far corner fills the cutback lane.
  - Safeties fill the alley.
- **Zone** (safeties, pass plays). A zone defender sits in his zone. He picks up the nearest receiver who enters it and carries him while the receiver stays in it. He breaks on the ball only when the catch point is inside his zone or he is the hawk. Zone radius scales with ZCV.
- **Man** (cover_man) was already a trail with cushion and inside leverage. Unchanged.
- **Still a beeline:** pursuit after a catch. `cover_man` and `zone` still switch to `pursue` when the carrier is set. This is a known gap.

Earlier in this pass, the trigger was "within 5 yd or past my depth minus 1". That made runs longer, because the far defenders converged late. It is now "within 7 yd or past the line".

### BDB replay harness (`scripts/bdb-physics-replay.mjs`)

- Each tracked player starts at his snap spot with his pre-snap velocity, and the formation holds until the snap (physics only, `ctx.snapState`).
- Scoring compares against tracked positions only after the simulation finishes. The engine never receives the play's yards, end spot, tackler, or post-snap positions.
- Coverage: 4-2-5 base against 11 personnel only. Of 3,672 week 1 to 5 run plays, 328 fit (9%).
- Ratings are position-group medians from the 2026 league file, because BDB 2022 players have no ratings in the repo.

## Open

1. **Tails in the physics path.** The nearest defender is about 1.2 m from the carrier when he crosses the line, and about 0.6 m a second later, so the defense converges. Carriers still get through, because the wrap and break-tackle odds in the physics tackle model are weak. This is the next physics tuning step.
2. **Replay gain error 90th percentile** went from 1.3 to 4.4 yd. Needs a look at which plays moved.
3. **Linebacker tracking** got worse at 1.0 s.
4. **Pursuit after a catch** is still a beeline with no reaction delay.
5. **Coverage** is narrow: 4-2-5 against 11 personnel, run plays only. No pass plays in the BDB replay.
