# Team-season playbook research build

Research-only output from `pipeline/build_playbooks.py --research`; no engine defaults or Week 8 snapshots were changed. The 2026 profiles are labeled **in-season, through Week 7**, frozen, and must not be used to tune tests or described as unseen. Participation inputs are unavailable for 2026 and are represented as missing, not zero.

## Reproduction

From a clean checkout with Python 3, run `python3 pipeline/fetch_research_playbook_sources.py` to verify the vendored 2026 Weeks 1–7 PBP and FTN snapshots and download the remaining inputs by URL and SHA-256 from `build_manifest.json`; then run `python3 pipeline/build_playbooks.py --research`. The fetcher and builder both refuse inputs whose SHA-256 differs from the manifest.
