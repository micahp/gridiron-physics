GRIDIRON SATURDAY

You have the ball. Ridgeline State against Coastal Tech, four 4-minute quarters. Call a play, hike it, then run with the stick: JUKE to make a tackler miss, STIFF to push one off, hold SPRINT to open up. On a pass, tap a number over a receiver to throw to him.

Keyboard: arrows/WASD move · Space hike/juke · E stiff arm · Shift sprint · 1-5 throw

Players: original sprite-stacked voxel models (add ?rig for the old 3D model, "American Football Player" by Hikky, CC BY 3.0). Sounds: Kenney (CC0), Gregor Quendel crowd (CC0).
